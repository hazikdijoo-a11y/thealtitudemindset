Pre-flight questions

Before you book.

What is NLP coaching?

NLP stands for Neuro-Linguistic Programming. In plain terms — Neuro is how your mind processes what you see, hear and feel. Linguistic is the language and self-talk you use to make sense of it. Programming is the pattern those two build over time: the automatic beliefs, reactions and habits you run on without noticing.

NLP coaching works with all three, so you can change the patterns that aren’t serving you. What was installed can be uninstalled.

Who is this coaching for?

Ambitious professionals and high-performing individuals who know they’re capable of more, but keep running into the same limiting beliefs, self-doubt, overthinking, emotional reactions or communication patterns. It’s for people who want practical change — not motivation.

Is this therapy?

No, and I’m careful about the difference. Coaching focuses on personal development — building confidence, breaking unhelpful patterns, working toward specific goals. It’s forward-looking and practical.

Diagnosing and treating clinical conditions such as depression, trauma or anxiety disorders is the work of licensed mental health professionals. If that’s what you need, I’ll say so and point you toward the right support.

What happens in the free discovery call?

It’s a free 30-minute conversation. We talk about what’s actually going on, what pattern might sit underneath it, and whether coaching is the right fit. There’s no pitch, and no obligation to book a paid session afterwards.

How much does coaching cost?

The 30-minute discovery call is free. One-to-one coaching sessions are ₹3,500 per session, booked one at a time — there’s no package you have to commit to upfront.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on how deep the limiting beliefs run and how big the goals are. Typically, meaningful shifts start showing within about four sessions.

Are sessions online or in person?

Online for everyone. If you’re based in Bengaluru, we can also meet in person.

Do you work with people outside Bengaluru?

Yes. Coaching runs online, so location isn’t a constraint — we just need a time that works in both our schedules.

What kinds of problems can we work on?

Limiting beliefs, self-doubt, low confidence, overthinking, fear of failure, emotional reactions, handling pressure, communication difficulties, relationship communication, and behavioural patterns that keep repeating.

What if I’m not sure coaching is right for me?

That’s exactly what the free call is for. If coaching isn’t the right fit, I’ll tell you — and if what you need is licensed mental health support instead, I’ll say that too.

Do you offer a refund?

Yes. If you cancel a paid session before it takes place, you get a full refund. And if you complete a session and genuinely feel you received no value from it, I refund that session in full. The details are in the refund policy.

Can I reschedule a session?

Yes — you can reschedule to any time that’s convenient for you and fits my availability.

Who certified you as an NLP Practitioner?

I’m certified as an NLP Practitioner and Life Coach through Auspicium (UK) and Transformation Academy (USA), and I practise under the ANLP Code of Ethics.

Where coaching isn’t the right tool

NLP coaching suits personal development and performance goals. It is not a substitute for licensed mental health care, and it does not treat clinical conditions. If any of the following apply, please seek support from a qualified mental health professional, instead of or alongside coaching:

  • Severe depression or suicidal thoughts
  • Psychosis or delusions
  • Active addiction requiring clinical treatment
  • PTSD or trauma requiring specialised therapy
  • Domestic violence or abuse situations
  • Any condition needing medical or licensed psychological care
