The real problem

You know you’re capable of more.

It usually isn’t intelligence. It isn’t talent, and it isn’t effort — you have already proved you have all three.

What’s actually happening is quieter than that. Somewhere along the way your mind built a pattern: a belief about what you’re capable of, a reaction that fires before you can think, a way of speaking to yourself that made sense once and doesn’t any more.

The pattern runs automatically. It made sense in the situation that created it. It just hasn’t been updated since.

That’s the thing worth working on — not your willpower.

Where it usually shows up

  • Overthinking every decision
  • Self-doubt that arrives on cue
  • Fear of failing publicly
  • Reacting emotionally, then regretting it
  • Struggling to say what you mean
  • Confidence that won’t hold under pressure
  • The same argument, on repeat
  • Knowing exactly what to do — and not doing it

If you recognised more than two of those, that isn’t a character flaw. It’s a pattern — and patterns can be worked with directly.

The method

The Altitude Mindset Framework™

Five stages that take you from noticing the pattern to living without it. Every coaching engagement follows this arc.

01

Recognize

Name the pattern. We find the specific belief, trigger or behaviour that keeps producing the same result — the one running underneath the situation you thought was the problem.

02

Reframe

Challenge the interpretation. Most limiting beliefs aren’t facts — they’re conclusions you drew once and never revisited. We take them apart and look at what else is true.

03

Reset

Change your state deliberately. You learn to shift out of a reactive emotional state and back into control — the skill that decides how you perform when pressure arrives.

04

Rewire

Build the new response. Using practical NLP techniques, we install thought patterns and behaviours that hold up in the moment, not just in theory.

05

Rise

Apply it in real life, consistently, until it stops being something you’re working on and becomes how you operate.

What changes

What you’ll actually walk away with.

Not abstractions. These are the six areas most of my clients come in for.

Limiting beliefs

You stop treating old conclusions as facts. The ceiling you assumed was real turns out to be something you can name, test and move.

Confidence

Confidence that doesn’t depend on the room going well — because it’s built on evidence and a repeatable internal state, not on mood.

Emotional mastery

You get the half-second back. Instead of reacting and reviewing it later, you notice the trigger, change state, and choose the response.

Communication

Say the thing you actually meant — clearly, without softening it into nothing or sharpening it into a fight. Rapport becomes a skill, not luck.

Relationships

The recurring argument stops recurring. You start seeing the pattern in the exchange instead of only the other person’s part in it.

Personal growth

Greater self-awareness, better decisions, and more consistent action — the gap between knowing and doing gets noticeably narrower.

What 10+ years in aviation taught me about people.

Most coaches study human behaviour. I was assessed on reading it correctly.

As a Line Check Cabin Crew Evaluator, my job was to observe crew during live operations — how they communicated, how they made decisions, how they held up when a situation stopped going to plan. Not in a workshop. In the air, with real consequences.

Ten years of that teaches you something specific: how someone behaves under pressure is not a personality trait. It’s a pattern. What I didn’t have then was a way to change them. NLP gave me that. The combination — years of watching real behaviour under real pressure, plus a practical toolkit for changing the pattern underneath it — is what makes this coaching different from advice.

Questions

Before you book.

What is NLP coaching?

NLP stands for Neuro-Linguistic Programming. In plain terms — Neuro is how your mind processes what you see, hear and feel. Linguistic is the language and self-talk you use to make sense of it. Programming is the pattern those two build over time: the automatic beliefs, reactions and habits you run on without noticing.

NLP coaching works with all three, so you can change the patterns that aren’t serving you. What was installed can be uninstalled.

Who is this coaching for?

Ambitious professionals and high-performing individuals who know they’re capable of more, but keep running into the same limiting beliefs, self-doubt, overthinking, emotional reactions or communication patterns. It’s for people who want practical change — not motivation.

Is this therapy?

No, and I’m careful about the difference. Coaching focuses on personal development — building confidence, breaking unhelpful patterns, working toward specific goals. It’s forward-looking and practical.

Diagnosing and treating clinical conditions such as depression, trauma or anxiety disorders is the work of licensed mental health professionals. If that’s what you need, I’ll say so and point you toward the right support.

What happens in the free discovery call?

It’s a free 30-minute conversation. We talk about what’s actually going on, what pattern might sit underneath it, and whether coaching is the right fit. There’s no pitch, and no obligation to book a paid session afterwards.

How much does coaching cost?

The 30-minute discovery call is free. One-to-one coaching sessions are ₹3,500 per session, booked one at a time — there’s no package you have to commit to upfront.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on how deep the limiting beliefs run and how big the goals are. Typically, meaningful shifts start showing within about four sessions.

Are sessions online or in person?

Online for everyone. If you’re based in Bengaluru, we can also meet in person.

Do you work with people outside Bengaluru?

Yes. Coaching runs online, so location isn’t a constraint — we just need a time that works in both our schedules.

What kinds of problems can we work on?

Limiting beliefs, self-doubt, low confidence, overthinking, fear of failure, emotional reactions, handling pressure, communication difficulties, relationship communication, and behavioural patterns that keep repeating.

What if I’m not sure coaching is right for me?

That’s exactly what the free call is for. If coaching isn’t the right fit, I’ll tell you — and if what you need is licensed mental health support instead, I’ll say that too.

Do you offer a refund?

If you complete your first paid session and genuinely feel you received no value from it, I offer a full refund.

Can I reschedule a session?

Yes — you can reschedule to any time that’s convenient for you and fits my availability.

Who certified you as an NLP Practitioner?

I’m certified as an NLP Practitioner and Life Coach through Auspicium (UK) and Transformation Academy (USA), and I practise under the ANLP Code of Ethics.

Where coaching isn’t the right tool

NLP coaching suits personal development and performance goals. It is not a substitute for licensed mental health care, and it does not treat clinical conditions. If any of the following apply, please seek support from a qualified mental health professional — instead of, or alongside, coaching.

  • Severe depression or suicidal thoughts
  • Psychosis or delusions
  • Active addiction requiring clinical treatment
  • PTSD or trauma requiring specialised therapy
  • Domestic violence or abuse situations
  • Any condition needing medical or licensed psychological care
