Is this for you?

Who I work best with.

Ambitious professionals and high performers who are already capable — and are tired of the same thing getting in the way.

  • You know what to do and still don’t do it
  • Your confidence doesn’t survive pressure
  • You overthink decisions long past useful
  • You react, then spend the evening reviewing it
  • You can’t say the thing you actually mean
  • The same argument keeps returning in a new costume

Honest boundaries

Who I don’t work with.

Coaching is for personal development and performance goals. It is not a substitute for licensed mental health care, and it does not treat clinical conditions. Please seek a qualified mental health professional — instead of, or alongside, coaching — for:

  • Severe depression or suicidal thoughts
  • Psychosis or delusions
  • Active addiction requiring clinical treatment
  • PTSD or trauma requiring specialised therapy
  • Domestic violence or abuse situations
  • Any condition needing medical or licensed psychological care

The journey

How coaching actually runs.

Structured, not open-ended. You always know which stage you’re in.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We understand the challenge properly and identify the pattern underneath it.

  2. 02

    Reframe

    We challenge the beliefs and interpretations holding the pattern in place.

  3. 03

    Rewire

    Practical NLP and mindset tools build a different response.

  4. 04

    Integrate

    You apply the new response to the real situations that used to catch you.

  5. 05

    Elevate

    It becomes consistent — and you no longer need me for it.
