About

What aviation taught me about people, NLP gave me the tools to change.

I’m Hazik Fayaz — a Certified NLP Practitioner and Life Coach based in Bengaluru, and a Line Check Cabin Crew Evaluator with more than ten years in commercial aviation.

My decade in aviation taught me one thing above everything else: how people behave under pressure is not a personality trait. It is a learnable, changeable skill.

As a Line Check Cabin Crew Evaluator, my job was to observe crew during live operations — conducting proficiency checks, assessing performance, and coaching cabin crew through high-stakes scenarios. Not in a classroom. In the air, where the consequences are real and nobody is performing for an assessor’s benefit for very long.

After enough years of that, you start seeing the same things repeat. And they weren’t really aviation problems.

Limiting beliefs that looked like confidence problems. Communication failures that were really rapport failures. Decisions made from fear, dressed up as logic.

I could see the patterns clearly. What I didn’t have was a way to change them — for the crew I was evaluating, or frankly for myself.

That’s what took me to NLP. Neuro-Linguistic Programming gave me a practical toolkit for the thing I’d spent a decade observing: the beliefs, reactions and internal language sitting underneath behaviour. I trained as an NLP Practitioner and Life Coach through Auspicium (UK) and Transformation Academy (USA), and I practise under the ANLP Code of Ethics.

Today I work with ambitious professionals who are in the same position those crew were — capable, driven, and quietly held back by a pattern that made sense once. Alongside the coaching, I’ve been building a storytelling voice across LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube and X, using aviation stories to make ideas about human behaviour concrete.

The combination is the point. Plenty of coaches have studied behaviour. Fewer have spent ten years being professionally accountable for reading it correctly while it was happening.
