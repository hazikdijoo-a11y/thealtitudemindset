I Visited the Taj Mahal Twice. The Second Time Changed Everything.

By Hazik Fayaz · November 19, 2026

I went to the Taj Mahal twice.

The first time

The first time, I went alone. Just to see it from the outside.

That day, it was a piece of art made years before. It had no purpose for me or for anyone else.

I looked, I walked around, and I left.

The second time

The second time, I thought I would explore it with friends, the same way.

But this time, my friends chose to take a guide.

I was sceptical for some time. But we finally agreed, and we got one.

The moment it changed

As soon as we entered the gates, the guide started describing every detail.

I was stunned.

My whole perspective about the Taj Mahal, and about life, changed right there.

There were so many hidden illusions. Scriptures engraved in the walls. And the architecture, of course, was amazing.

Details I had walked right past the first time.

Same place. Different experience.

The Taj Mahal hadn’t changed between my two visits.

The walls were the same. The engravings were the same. The architecture was the same.

The only difference was that someone showed me how to see it.

My only takeaway

When you see your life from a coach’s perspective, you are able to get insights into all the details you need to know in order to navigate your life.

Think about your own life

How much of your life are you walking past, the way I walked past the Taj Mahal?

  • The patterns in your relationships that you’ve never named
  • The strengths that have been there all along
  • The habits quietly shaping your direction
  • The “scriptures” written into your own story that you’ve never stopped to read

They’re not hidden because they’re missing. They’re hidden because no one has pointed them out.

What a guide does

A guide doesn’t build the monument for you.

A guide doesn’t change the walls.

A guide shows you what was already there, and teaches you where to look.

That is what a coach does.

The lesson

I almost refused the guide. I thought I could see it all on my own.

I’m glad I was wrong.

You can look at your life alone, and it may seem like a beautiful but purposeless structure.

Or you can see it through someone who helps you notice the details.

The details were always there.

Sometimes we just need someone to show us where to look.
