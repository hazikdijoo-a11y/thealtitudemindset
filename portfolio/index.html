    How a project runs

    One person, first idea to live release

    1. Shape

      Start from the problem. Users, scope and what “done” means are written down before anything is built, then designed with every state, not just the happy path.

    2. Build

      Frontend, backend and integrations in working slices you can click through early, not one big reveal at the end.

    3. Prove

      Automated tests plus real walkthroughs, including a real payment and refund. What isn’t verified is said plainly.

    4. Ship and improve

      Deploy, watch what real people do, fix it, and keep a written list of what’s still left.
