Process

How I build

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the problem, the people who’ll use the product and the business model behind it.

  2. 02

    Define

    Turn the idea into a clear product structure: screens, data, roles and what’s in the first release.

  3. 03

    Design

    Design the UI/UX and user flows, including loading, empty and error states, not just the happy path.

  4. 04

    Build

    Develop the frontend, backend and integrations in working slices you can click through early.

  5. 05

    Test

    Check functionality, responsiveness, security and usability, and say plainly what is and isn’t verified.

  6. 06

    Launch

    Deploy, monitor, and keep improving the product once real people use it.
