Why this exists

Most aspiring cabin crew candidates in India are confused by conflicting information — one page says you need a degree, another says a ₹50,000 course is compulsory, a third says height doesn't matter at all. Meanwhile, recruitment scams target exactly this confusion with fake offer letters and "security deposit" demands. This session replaces the guesswork with what's actually published, and a clear way to protect yourself from the rest.
