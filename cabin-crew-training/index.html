Straight answers

Common questions

Will this guarantee I get selected?

No — and be careful with anyone who says otherwise. Airlines weigh many factors, several of which are outside anyone’s control. What I focus on is making sure you walk in prepared and genuinely confident: grooming, communication and group discussion technique, presented as your best self.

What age group is this for?

The training is designed for candidates aged 18 to 26.5 years, matching typical airline eligibility criteria.

What qualifies you to teach this?

More than ten years in commercial aviation as cabin crew and as a Line Check Cabin Crew Evaluator — conducting proficiency checks and assessing crew performance — plus faculty experience at Viinzs Airhostess Training Academy in Srinagar.

Is this the same as your NLP coaching?

No. This is practical interview and selection preparation. My NLP and mindset coaching is a separate practice for professionals working on confidence, limiting beliefs and behavioural patterns — you can read about that here. Some of the mindset tools overlap; the purpose doesn’t.
