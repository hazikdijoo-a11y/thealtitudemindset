A New Project: ShiftReset

By Hazik Fayaz · August 13, 2026

Most of what I write here is about mindset and how you think under pressure — but there's a physical side to performance that doesn't get enough attention: sleep.

That's especially true for people whose work schedule fights their body clock — nurses, truckers, warehouse and factory workers, first responders, pilots and cabin crew. My own aviation background made this one personal; fatigue management isn't an afterthought in flight ops, it's part of the job. Most sleep advice online assumes a normal 9-to-5, and it just doesn't hold up for people working nights or rotating shifts.

So I built ShiftReset — a free site with a Sleep Debt Calculator and practical, role-specific guides for people navigating exactly that. It's a different niche from what I usually cover here, but the underlying idea is the same one I talk about with clients all the time: you can't perform on a system that's running on empty.

If you work nights or rotating shifts, or know someone who does, it's worth a look: shiftreset.in
